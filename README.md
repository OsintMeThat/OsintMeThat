# OSINT Me That

Open-source investigations into conflicts and geopolitical events: geolocation,
chronolocation, satellite imagery, and the method behind every finding.

**Read them on [osintmethat.com](https://osintmethat.com)** · [RSS](https://osintmethat.com/feed.xml)

- **Investigations**: reconstructions built on verified open-source data, such as
  [Operation Absolute Resolve](https://osintmethat.com/2026/03/12/operation-absolute-resolve-the-night-maduro-was-taken-a-full-osint-reconstruction/).
- **Conflict Watch**: front lines and military movements on maps, such as
  [the fall of the Bab al-Mandab front](https://osintmethat.com/bab-el-mandab-2026/).
- **Methods & Tools**: how the work is done, and the tools it is done with.

## Azimut

[Azimut](https://github.com/OsintMeThat/azimut) is the workbench these investigations
are made with. One case, one folder, every tool, and nothing leaves your machine.
