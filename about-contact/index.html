About

One analyst, open sources, and a map

I geolocate and date videos and photos from conflicts, count what they show, and publish it with every source linked, so anyone can check the work.

How I work

  1. 01Sources firstEvery claim links to the post, the video or the image it comes from.
  2. 02Place and timeA video counts once it is matched to satellite imagery and dated.
  3. 03Figures are minimumsA count covers only what was seen and placed, so it reads “at least”.
  4. 04Methods in the openThe Methods & Tools articles show how it was done, step by step.
