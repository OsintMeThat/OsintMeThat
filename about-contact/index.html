About OsintMeThat

OsintMeThat is an independent digital investigation platform dedicated to uncovering the truth through Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) and Geospatial Intelligence (GEOINT).

In an era of unprecedented information warfare, our mission is to cut through the noise of disinformation and provide clear, evidence-based analysis on global conflicts and geopolitical events.

Our Approach

We believe that transparency is the key to credibility. Every investigation published on this platform follows a strict rigorous process:

  • Verification: We never take a claim at face value. Every piece of visual or textual evidence is cross-referenced.
  • Geolocation & Chronolocation: Using satellite imagery, street view data, and shadows analysis, we pinpoint the "where" and "when" of critical events.
  • Neutrality: Our goal is not to take sides, but to document facts. We analyze networks of influence and disinformation regardless of their origin.

What We Track

  • Conflict Monitoring: Real-time tracking of frontlines and military movements using interactive mapping.
  • Disinformation Networks: Mapping bot farms, fake social media accounts, and influence operations.
  • Digital Forensic: Deep-dives into manipulated media, AI-generated content, and metadata analysis.

Methodology & Ethics

At OsintMeThat, we don't just show you the result; we show you the path. Most of our reports include a "Methods & Tools" section, allowing our readers to understand our workflow and replicate our findings. We are committed to ethical OSINT practices, ensuring that our investigations serve the public interest without compromising personal privacy or safety.

Why this platform?

In a world of fragmented information and digital noise, clarity is the ultimate tool. I created OsintMeThat to bridge the gap between raw data and actionable understanding. Driven by a passion for geography and mapping, I use open-source intelligence to verify facts, track events, and provide an unbiased view of global conflicts. This platform is more than just a blog—it is a commitment to evidence, transparency, and the belief that the truth is always out there, waiting to be mapped.
