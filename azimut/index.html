The OSINT workspace that runs on your machine

A toolkit for open-source investigations, strongest at geolocation. Each investigation is a case: a folder on your own computer where everything you do is filed. I build it and give it away.

Free and open source (AGPL-3.0) · Windows, macOS, Linux

Download the latest versionSource on GitHub

From the first video to the published map

01 · Collect

Collect

Every picture, video and file of the case, each with where it came from.

A browser add-on files what you look at in another tab into the open case, and draws Azimut’s tools over the maps you already use.

Media · Capture extension

02 · Examine

Examine

One image or one video, read closely: frames, adjustments, metadata and error levels.

A picture or a frame is prepared for the reverse-search engines. Nothing is sent until you press one.

Inspect · Reverse search · Collage

03 · Geolocate

Geolocate

Imagery of anywhere, from free and keyed providers, to search, measure and capture; two locked views of the same ground, read side by side, by swipe, fade or blink.

Horizon stands you on the ground and draws what you would see. Find ranks the places whose relief fits the skyline of a photo, for you to check one by one.

Satellite · Compare · Horizon · Find

04 · Date and detect

Date and detect

Any coordinate read in every notation, with the sun and moon over it on a date.

Detect sweeps ground in Sentinel imagery for change, once or on every new pass.

Coordinates & sky · Detect

05 · Connect

Connect

The case as an index: people, accounts, places and things first, events and files below.

Every dated statement and file of the case on one axis, in the place’s own time.

Board · Timeline · Graph · Sheets

06 · Prove and publish

Prove and publish

Pictures side by side, what agrees marked, the point it proves filed; notes written in place, citing what the case holds.

A page laid out round the case’s maps, exported as an image, a PDF or a video, and the sourced thread that publishes a finding. Pressing Post is left to you.

Proof · Notebook · Map plates · Post · Web maps

Install

  1. 01Ready to runDownload the file for your system from the latest release and open it. It opens in your browser; nothing else to install.
  2. 02With Pythonpipx install azimut, then azimut.

Each release changes a detail or two, so the full guide (first-run warnings, updates, building from source) lives with the code, in the README, and stays current. Your cases live in a folder of their own: updating or removing Azimut never touches them.
