The Bab al-Mandab front on one map

Every vehicle left on the road and every video geolocated on this front, September to November 2026. Read the full analysis.

MPGeoint@LoLManya

@LoLManya

Press a vehicle to see the frame it was identified from, and a camera to see the proof of a geolocation with its source video.

The yellow line is the furthest the government forces can be shown to have reached before the withdrawal of 15 November. It is an approximate maximum, not a front held every day.

Every figure is a minimum: it covers only the geolocated footage and the two Houthi videos of 15 and 16 November.

Made with Azimut 0.3.4, in development, a free, open-source OSINT workbench.
