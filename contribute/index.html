Send an investigation made with Azimut

OSINT Me That publishes open-source investigations, placed on the map. Yours can be one of them: send it from Azimut, it is read, you get a private preview, and it is published under your name once you say yes.

Send your article ↓How it works

Why send it

  • Your name and pictureThe article and its maps carry both, on a site read by people who follow open-source investigation.
  • Read by an analyst firstNothing goes online before that, and any question comes to you first.
  • Maps that stay liveThey stay interactive, and an article with a place gets its pin on the home page's world map.
  • Shared on X and DiscordEach published article goes out on X and on the Discord.

What is accepted

  • Made in AzimutAn investigation made in Azimut, its article written in the case's Notebook.
  • One .azimut.zip fileThe file Azimut's Export › For review writes, from Azimut 0.3.4 on. Never a web page or a folder of HTML: nothing runs on this site that was not built for it.
  • Open sourcesOpen sources only, each one linked from the article.

How

  1. 01Write the articleIn your case's Notebook, its sources linked and its maps beside it.
  2. 02Export › For reviewOpen the note, press Export and pick For review.
  3. 03Check who signs itThe name and the picture there are the ones the article and its maps will show.
  4. 04SendWith the form below.
