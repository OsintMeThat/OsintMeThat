Operation Absolute Resolve, the map

Every located point of the night of 3 January 2026 in Venezuela, on one interactive map. Read the full analysis.

MPGeoint@LoLManya

@LoLManya

Press a point to see what it shows, its sources and the post that geolocates it. A camera is a point of view, where a video or a photo was taken from, and a crossed marker is something destroyed or damaged.

Search for a kind of gear in the box, for example MH-60, MH-47 or Buk-M2E.

Only the points that could be geolocated are on the map.

Made with Azimut 0.3.4, in development, a free, open-source OSINT workbench.
