Privacy

This site sets no cookies and has no accounts, forms or newsletter.

Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which stores nothing on your device: your browser sends Cloudflare the page's address and how it loaded, and the site sees totals only.

GitHub, which hosts the site, logs visitors' IP addresses for security, as its documentation says.

Videos, posts and maps from YouTube, Instagram and Google load only when you press them, and their own privacy policies apply from then on.

A message sent by e-mail stays in that mailbox and is used only to answer it.

For any question about your data, write to osintmethat@proton.me. You can also complain to the CNIL.
